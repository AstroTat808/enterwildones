Physical iPhone certification

Run these four checks on real devices.

Use controlled test guests only. Record the result in the checklist below immediately after each successful check.

1Apple Wallet

Open a controlled paid ticket on your iPhone after its waiver is signed. Tap Add to Apple Wallet. Confirm the pass opens, shows the correct realm/event/guest, and remains available with connectivity disabled. Then mark Apple Wallet Passed.

2Gate scan

On a second staff device open Check-In, select the same realm, sign in, and scan the Wallet pass or ticket QR. The first scan must check the guest in; the second scan must be rejected as already checked in. Mark Gate scan Passed.

3Bartender scan

Use a checked-in controlled ticket with an active drink package. Open Bar, scan the ticket, activate a test wristband, redeem one credit, then verify the credit count decreases exactly once. If possible repeat from a second bartender device. Mark Bartender redemption Passed.

4Refund test

Use a separate fresh controlled admission or unused add-on that has not been checked in or activated. Refund it from Operations, confirm Stripe reports success, and verify the local ticket/entitlement becomes refunded. Mark Refund Passed.

Important: do not use the gate/bartender test ticket for the refund test. Checked-in admissions and activated/used drink packages are intentionally blocked from self-service admin refunds and require manual review.
