# Legal evidence operations — release gate

**Status:** draft implementation, not production approved. Counsel and the insurer must approve the agreement wording, the retention schedule, privacy notice, permitted evidence access, incident categories, and procedures before commissioning. Do not assume any specific period of retention is legally required. No AWS resources have been created by this change.

## Independent storage commissioning

Use two AWS accounts controlled by separate authorized custodians, with dedicated general-purpose S3 buckets in different regions. Enable Object Lock, versioning, Block Public Access, bucket-owner-enforced ownership, default encryption, TLS-only access, and CloudTrail data events to an independently retained audit destination. Configure a counsel-approved default Compliance retention horizon matching the explicitly approved application retention policy. Do not use actual guest records for commissioning.

The application uses conditional PUT with per-object COMPLIANCE retention, AES256 server-side encryption, Content-MD5, expected owner and SHA-256 verification. It retrieves each exact version after writing and checks the retention metadata, encryption, and bytes. It stores two pinned version receipts locally. It never deletes an object, removes a legal hold, or shortens retention. A successful local receipt does not imply continuous live S3 availability; the recovery drill verifies availability separately. The local Netlify archive and receipt index are still application-controlled.

Grant distinct least-privilege writer/read-verifier identities for each account, restricted to that account's evidence/v1/* resource prefix. Permit s3:PutObject, s3:PutObjectRetention (necessary when specifying retention on upload), s3:GetObject, s3:GetObjectVersion, s3:GetObjectRetention and s3:GetObjectLegalHold. Deny deletion, retention bypass, legal-hold changes, bucket configuration, ACL changes and other administrative operations. Use bucket policies to require TLS, expected account boundary, encryption, Compliance mode, and approved minimum/maximum retention days; test them with synthetic data. In addition, deny writes without the conditional If-None-Match header where supported. Do not use root or administrator credentials in Netlify. Use separately managed credentials and rotation; IAM policies and actual bucket settings must be reviewed by the AWS custodians.

Set the WILDONES_WORM_PRIMARY_* and BACKUP_* variables from .env.example as secret, Functions-only variables, scoped to the specific deployment context. Set WILDONES_WORM_RETENTION_DAYS only with written approval. Production requires independent archival; preview does not send evidence to AWS unless specifically configured for isolated testing. Keep preview data synthetic and preview accounts separate. Do not put credentials in source, logs, emails or chats.

Run the scheduled waiver-archive-drill function using synthetic data and verify both pinned version receipts in the actual AWS accounts. Confirm the recovery timestamp and configuration fingerprint in Launch Readiness. Drill runs daily at 08:30 HST and verifies a synthetic canary plus a rotating bounded subset of actual receipts. Recovery health expires after 36 hours and after any target/retention policy change. A successful sample is not a full corpus audit. For a full reconciliation, enumerate all authoritative historical signatures, including revoked/refunded/completed-event tickets, and all archived digests; use the per-ticket archive reconciliation page to preserve each record, verify both pinned copies, and reconcile totals. Never backdate, synthesize, or re-sign on a guest's behalf. Historical records lacking the original exact text remain metadata-only. Retention expiry and index loss require separately documented recovery using S3 Inventory with version IDs and retained export manifests; do not treat a missing receipt as a missing signature without investigation.

## Legal holds

Legal holds are independent of retention dates. The website intentionally has no permission to release or apply legal holds. Custodians use separate identities with narrowly scoped s3:PutObjectLegalHold, s3:GetObjectLegalHold and version-specific read permissions, not application credentials. The command-line helper scripts/apply-waiver-legal-hold.mjs accepts an authenticated evidence export, validates its record digest and both pinned object versions, prints a dry-run, and only applies ON when explicitly executed. Run independently for primary and backup AWS profiles. Verify each exact version afterward; if a hold succeeds on one copy but fails on another, preserve the successful hold and escalate immediately. Preserve the command results in the restricted case file. Removal requires documented legal approval and an independent custodian workflow and is deliberately not supported by this helper. Never apply a hold based solely on a guest name; verify the exact case, event, ticket, digest and version IDs first.

## Evidence and incident administration

From Master Admin, use Legal Waivers, Incident Register and Waiver Re-sign Campaign. All use existing shared admin session/bearer authorization. This is not individual identity or per-case RBAC; limit the admin secret to trusted legal administrators until individual MFA, RBAC and access attribution are implemented. Incident reporter name is self-reported. Do not enter medical diagnoses, IDs, or unnecessary sensitive personal information; document observed facts, time, location, response and relevant ticket identity. Provide emergency assistance first. Corrections are appended, not overwritten. A remote failure can leave a local pending incident record; retry its archive instead of creating a second report. Export only to authorized recipients and protect the downloaded files.

Signed PDFs reproduce the supported text using a standard PDF font and ToUnicode mapping, plus signature metadata, text hash and evidence hash. A PDF is an electronic-signature evidence copy, not a certificate-based digital signature. Unsupported glyphs fail closed with an exact Unicode HTML/print or original JSON alternative; no name transliteration. Historical metadata-only records cannot produce a complete signed PDF. Admin evidence exports independently retrieve and verify both remote versions when WORM is required. Use the original JSON for forensic verification. Incident evidence exports include related waiver records and scoped application audit records; those audit entries are not independently immutable.

## Re-sign campaign

The preview identifies current active named admissions missing a current participant or required supplemental waiver, masks email addresses, and excludes already-current, inactive, expired-token and mismatched-identity records. It does not send. Sending requires the exact event and combined waiver hash approval in WILDONES_WAIVER_RESIGN_APPROVED_EVENT and WILDONES_WAIVER_RESIGN_APPROVED_VERSION, production context, fresh recovery health, the event not yet begun, and explicit SEND confirmation for each ticket. Recheck eligibility before sending. Resend idempotency is version/ticket bound; provider acceptance is not delivery. Verify actual delivery/failed attempts in Email Health. Do not bulk-send without a reconciled recipient audit. Existing pre-event reminders remain separate. Communicate a review/remedy path for guests who decline changed terms.

## Release checklist

- Legal and insurance review of both entities and agreement versions; approve data handling, storage geography and retention/hold policy.
- Configure and verify both private S3 accounts, encryption, access policies, version pinning, conditional writes, failed replica recovery, restore proof and custodian legal-hold dry run.
- Reconcile all historical records without changing original consent. Test both PDF and original JSON with synthetic multilingual and incomplete legacy cases; review the rendered PDF and text extraction.
- Certify the exact PR head in build, release, Chromium/WebKit, mobile and Netlify preview checks. Rehearse paid, complimentary, bundled/standalone late stay, stale and tampered evidence, refunded/disputed tickets, offline handling, physical scanner and Apple Wallet. Confirm the ticket holders' re-sign message and remedy process.
- Schedule a controlled rollout before event day. Do not merge until the legal and operational release gates are satisfied. Do not change live payment settings, capacity, or guest records as part of testing.
